Cash Flow Statement
The Cash Flow Statement examines what happened in
the Cash Account during the year. Cash injections appear as positive
numbers and cash withdrawals as negative numbers. The Cash Flow
Statement is an excellent tool for diagnosing emergency loans. When
negative cash flows exceed positives, you are forced to seek emergency
funding. For example, if sales are bad and you find yourself carrying an
abundance of excess inventory, the report would show the increase in
inventory as a huge negative cash flow. Too much unexpected inventory
could outstrip your inflows, exhaust your starting cash and force you to beg
for money to keep your company afloat.
